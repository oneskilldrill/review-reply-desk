How it works

  1. You email us the public link to your reviews (Google Business Profile, Yelp or similar). No login needed.
  2. We send 3 free sample replies to your own unanswered reviews, so you can judge the voice before paying anything.
  3. Want the rest? We draft a reply for each review that needs one, matched to your tone and how your business works, usually within 48 hours. Every draft is marked "DRAFT, not approved", and tough reviews come with a few questions so we get the facts right.
  4. You approve every reply: edit it, okay it, or skip it. You reply "approved", and only then do we send the invoice.
  5. You copy each approved reply and post it from your own account. On the monthly plan, we keep drafting replies to new reviews from your public page.

Questions

Do you log in to my profile or post for me?

No. We never ask for your password or for owner or manager access, and we never post. We send drafts. You approve, edit or skip each one, then post it from your own account.

Is my Google Business Profile free?

Yes. Google Business Profile is free to use. You'd only pay us for drafting replies.

Are you Google?

No. Review Reply Desk is an independent service from One Skill Drill and isn't affiliated with Google, Yelp or any review platform. Nobody can promise you a better ranking on Google, and we don't.

What about angry or unfair reviews?

We keep the public reply short and calm, avoid admitting fault, and point the customer to a phone call or visit so it gets handled privately.

Will replies sound copy-pasted?

No. Each reply responds to what that customer wrote, as in the examples above.

Working with a third party: Google's notice

Google asks third parties that help small businesses with their Business Profile to share its disclosure notice: Tips for working with third parties to manage your Business Profile. In short, Google says:

  • Business Profile is a tool you can use at no charge. Third parties often charge a fee for services like responding to reviews.
  • Third parties can't promise or directly influence your ranking on Google Search or Maps.
  • Third parties can't remove your business from Google or access your profile without your permission. Only profile owners control who has access.
  • Before you agree to work with a third party, get a written copy of the services and their costs.

Google's full rules for third parties are here: Business Profile third-party policies. Since we only draft and you post, we never hold access to your profile.
